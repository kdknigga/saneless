<objective>
Make `classify_source()` in `scanner/base.py` the ONLY source-classification rule in the
codebase, and land the routing correction that follows from it.

Purpose: two rules disagree today. `sane_backend._is_adf_source` tests `"adf" in

stack instead of one page. That is the C-06 fix.

Output: `SourceKind` + `classify_source()` + `SourceKind.uses_feeder` in `scanner/base.py`,
both existing rules delegating to it, and a test that could not have passed before this phase.
</objective>
