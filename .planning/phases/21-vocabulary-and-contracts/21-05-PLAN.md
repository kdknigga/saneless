`run_pipeline`'s `-> dict` that nobody reads with a typed `ScanResult`. Then correct the one
documentation sentence that claims a job status which does not exist.

`run_pipeline` is invisible to `ty` and `pyrefly` — a typo in `"fallback"` at either end
silently selects the wrong branch, and the pipeline's return value is an untyped dict that
both callers discard.

from `src/` and `tests/`, and one honest sentence in the docs.
