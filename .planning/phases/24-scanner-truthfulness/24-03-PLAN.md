<objective>
Stop reporting every first-page failure as an empty feeder, and bound the ADF iteration loop.

Purpose: `sane_backend.py:403-406` converts *any* exception on page 0 into `FeederEmptyError`, so a
jam, an open cover, a busy device and an I/O error all tell the operator to load paper (M-11). And
python-sane's `_SaneIterator.__next__` stops only on the exact string
`"Document feeder out of documents"`, so on non-feeder hardware `start()`/`snap()` keep succeeding,
the per-page timeout is satisfied every iteration, and `pipeline.py` materialises the pages with
`list()` — W-01's unbounded loop, reproduced live during research with a Flatbed source yielding ≥12
pages and refusing to stop.

Output: `_scan_adf_pages` split into two helpers, the first-page special case and the unreachable
guard deleted, `_MAX_ADF_PAGES` added, and the Phase 21 security register's false accept rationale
corrected.
</objective>

class _SaneIterator:

        try:
            self.device.start()
            return self.device.snap(True)
        except Exception as e:
