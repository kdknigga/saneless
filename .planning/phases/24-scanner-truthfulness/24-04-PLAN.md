<objective>
Stop the scanner backend deciding what a page is worth.

Purpose: `_validate_page_image` discards any page with mean > 254 and stddev < 1 regardless of the
profile's `enable_empty_page_detection` toggle. In Lineart a clean blank page is exactly mean 255.0 /
stddev 0.0, so it is dropped before the pipeline sees it — breaking manual-duplex parity and making
the documented "keep all pages" setting untrue (M-14). Research proved it end to end: the SANE `test`
backend's default picture is solid black, so **all ten** pages of the ten-page ADF stack are
discarded today and `scan_pages` returns an empty list.

Output: the pure-white and pure-black checks and their four constants are deleted; integrity failures
skip and count with an all-rejected raise; the "keep all pages" documentation becomes true; and
SCNR-08's ten-page assertion turns green, letting CI run the `sane_hardware` marker.
</objective>
