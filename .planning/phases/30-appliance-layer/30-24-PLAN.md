<objective>
Make the shutdown bound the comment claims the bound that shutdown actually has.

Purpose: `app.py:255-263` asserts "Both stop events are set before either join begins, so the two
bounded joins overlap and the worst case stays `STOP_JOIN_SECONDS` instead of doubling", and
`CheckRefresher.request_stop`'s docstring repeats it. The joins are sequential: `refresher.stop()`
runs only after `worker.stop()` returns and starts its own clock then. The early signal makes the
claim true only when the refresher winds down inside the window -- and the pathological case the
bound exists for is precisely the one where it does not, a refresher parked inside an unbounded
`getaddrinfo` (WR-02) or inside `sane_get_devices`. A container stop grace period sized on the
documented bound is then half of what it needs to be.
Output: a shared deadline, an optional join bound on the refresher, and two docstrings that stop
overstating.
</objective>
