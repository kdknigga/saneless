<objective>
Collapse `prune()`'s two `COUNT(*)` reads straddling two `DELETE`s into one `DELETE` that reports its own `rowcount`, and pin the SQLite behaviour that makes the collapse correct.

Purpose: STOR-04's second half and ROADMAP criterion 5's second half. The current shape (`job.py:277` and `:292` around deletes at `:281` and `:286`) has a race N-17 documents: a concurrent `create_job` between the two counts made the result wrong, and a scratch test forced it to return `-1`. Plan 22-03's lock closes the in-process race; the single statement removes the arithmetic that made it possible at all.

Output: a single-statement `prune()` in `job.py` plus two regression tests that convert an undocumented SQLite query-plan dependency into a loud failure.
</objective>
