<objective>
Make the worker loop unkillable and honest about its health (ROBU-01, D-10..D-13): guard the loop,
split job failures from loop failures, degrade after repeated store failures, heal through an
idle-tick store probe, and move prune out of the job path.

Purpose: today any store error in `update_state`, the failure-path `finish_job`, or the per-job
`prune` ends the thread silently and every later scan hangs PENDING. Research Pitfall 6: a job
whose final write failed would sit "Scanning..." with a disabled button until restart, so recovery
also reconciles those rows -- using only texts that already exist (the failure text the guard tried
to write, or RESTART_REASON for rows left by a failed startup recovery). `/health` consumes `health`
in 26-10; the lifespan calls `mark_recovery_pending()` in 26-09.

Output: guarded loop, `health`, `mark_recovery_pending`, idle housekeeping, tests; the two prune
tests rewritten.
</objective>
