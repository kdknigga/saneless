<objective>
Close CR-01, the phase's one blocking gap: make a flip answer belong to exactly one job, and make it
count only once that job is actually waiting at the flip prompt.

Purpose: today `_process_job` builds the `WorkerFlipCoordinator` at job start (`worker.py:256`) and
`continue_flip`/`abort_flip` (`worker.py:171-183`) answer whichever coordinator exists. The verifier
reproduced it against the real worker and pipeline: a double-clicked Abort meant for job 1 aborted
job 2's pass A, and a stale early Continue starts pass B on an unflipped stack (C-02, the failure this
phase exists to close). That falsifies DPLX-06's "Abort at the flip prompt cancels the job".

This tightens D-16 ("the coordinator answers once, and its answer is final") to also be
**job-scoped and window-scoped**: an answer counts only if it names the job that is waiting, and only
once that job has announced `AWAITING_FLIP`. D-09 is untouched (the ABC keeps its one abstract
method `wait_for_flip(timeout) -> FlipOutcome`; arming is a `WorkerFlipCoordinator` detail, not a
change to the contract). D-17's shared `_current_or_recent_job` lookup is untouched.

Output: a job-bound, armed coordinator; job-scoped worker signals that report claimed/dropped; routes
that require the job id; a flip partial that sends it; and regression tests that reproduce the
verifier's scenario against the real `run_pipeline`.

Out of scope here (other gap plans): the route rendering an acknowledgment instead of the stale flip
prompt (25-11), the shared single-answer helper IN-02 (25-14), the docs and deferred-items correction
(25-15).
</objective>
