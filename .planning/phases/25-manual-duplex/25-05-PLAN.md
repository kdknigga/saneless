<objective>
Delete the `_transition_event` protocol in full, make pass B observable from outside the worker,
give all four route sites the same job lookup, and prove the flip timeout unparks the worker.

Purpose: DPLX-06's deletion clause and DPLX-05's release clause. `routes.py:297` is the **only**
external reader of `_transition_event`; once plan 25-02 gave `SCANNING_REVERSE` a real state, the
event would be written five times and read never — dead machinery a future reader would mistake for
load-bearing. It also blocks the event loop for 2 s on every flip.

Output: a smaller worker, one route helper at four call sites, and the e2e proof that a timeout
releases the thread.
</objective>
